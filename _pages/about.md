## The Firm

**Desert Springs Civil Engineering PLLC** operates at the intersection of traditional civil engineering practice and open-source infrastructure technology.

### What Makes Us Different

**Engineers building tools for engineers.**

Most civil engineering software is built by software companies, then sold to engineers. We're doing it the other way around.

Desert Springs Civil brings real project experience to every line of code. When our software calculates a vertical curve or exports an alignment, it's built by someone who's staked alignments in the field and reviewed shop drawings at 2am.

### Our Approach

- **Personalized Service** — Direct access to the engineer throughout your project
- **Broad Experience** — From construction inspection to federal infrastructure
- **Modern Tools** — AI and cutting-edge design software where appropriate
- **Open Standards** — We believe in interoperability and data accessibility
