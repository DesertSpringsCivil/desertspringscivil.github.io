## Saikei Civil

Our flagship project: **native IFC 4.3 infrastructure design software**.

Built on Blender and IfcOpenShell, Saikei Civil creates infrastructure designs that are interoperable from day one—not converted after the fact.

### Why Native IFC?

Most civil design software exports to IFC as an afterthought. Saikei Civil is different: your design *is* the IFC model. No translation, no data loss, no broken references.

### Current Capabilities

- **Horizontal Alignments** — PI-based design with arc, spiral, and tangent segments
- **Vertical Profiles** — PVI-based design with parabolic vertical curves
- **Cross-Sections** — AASHTO-compliant lane and shoulder templates
- **Georeferencing** — Full coordinate system support via PyProj (any EPSG code)
- **Native IFC 4.3 Export** — True infrastructure classes, not workarounds

### Project Stats

**30,000+ lines** of production code | **107+ tests** passing | **GPL v3** licensed

### Built on Open Standards

Saikei Civil leverages proven open-source foundations:

- **Blender** — 3D modeling and visualization
- **IfcOpenShell** — IFC reading and writing
- **PyProj** — Coordinate transformations
- **buildingSMART IFC 4.3** — The open standard for infrastructure
