Water is the foundation of civil infrastructure. Desert Springs Civil Engineering brings 14+ years of experience in stormwater, irrigation, and floodplain engineering to every project.

---

## Stormwater Management

Design and analysis of drainage systems to manage runoff effectively and meet regulatory requirements.

- Detention/retention pond design
- Storm drain systems
- Low impact development (LID) solutions
- MS4 permit compliance
- Green infrastructure integration

---

## Hydrology & Hydraulic Studies

Site analysis for water impacts, flood modeling, and drainage assessments.

- Watershed delineation
- Peak flow calculations (rational method, SCS/NRCS methods)
- Flood routing analysis
- Channel hydraulics (HEC-RAS)
- Culvert sizing and analysis

---

## FEMA Floodplain Permitting

Navigate federal floodplain regulations with expert guidance.

- LOMR/CLOMR applications
- Floodplain development permits
- No-rise certifications
- Floodway analysis
- Base flood elevation determination

---

## Irrigation Design

Optimize water delivery systems for agricultural and residential applications.

- Gravity irrigation systems
- Pressurized irrigation design
- Water rights analysis
- System efficiency improvements
- Lateral and mainline sizing

---

## Well & Septic Systems

Design services for rural and residential water and wastewater systems.

- Well site evaluation
- Septic system design
- Soil and site analysis
- Permitting support (DEQ coordination)
